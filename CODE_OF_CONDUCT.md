# Code of Conduct

## The short version

Be decent. Assume the other person is trying to help.

This is a small project. Disagreement about technical decisions is welcome and useful —
several of the rules in `CLAUDE.md` exist because someone pushed back. Disagreement about
whether someone deserves to be treated with respect is not.

## Not acceptable

- Harassment, insults, or personal attacks
- Sexualised language or imagery, and unwelcome advances
- Publishing anyone's private information without their permission
- Sustained disruption of discussions

## Scope

This applies in issues, pull requests, commit messages, and anywhere else someone is
representing the project.

## Reporting

Please **do not** report a conduct problem in a public thread.

The maintainer is [@kbsiy0](https://github.com/kbsiy0). Open an issue titled
"private contact request" with no details in it, and I'll follow up privately. If the person
you need to report is the maintainer, GitHub Support can escalate it.

I'll handle reports myself. Depending on what happened, that may mean a warning, editing or
removing a comment, or blocking an account. I'll tell you what I did.
